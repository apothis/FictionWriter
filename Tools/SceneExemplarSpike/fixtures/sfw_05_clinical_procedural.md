Arrived on scene 04:12. Lead detective Garner had secured the perimeter; tape was at the property line and three uniformed officers were maintaining the cordon. Weather: light rain, ambient 9°C. Photography had been completed by Officer Voss prior to my arrival; her log indicated 144 frames at six angles. I requested the additional set noted on the supplementary sheet.

Decedent was male, approximately 50 to 60 years of age, found supine in the entryway with feet toward the door and head approximately 80 cm from the bottom step. Livor was fixed and consistent with the present position. Rigor was complete in the small muscles of the face and the larger muscles of the upper extremities; lower extremities not yet assessable through clothing.

Time of death estimation, preliminary: 6 to 10 hours prior to discovery. I noted ambient temperature in the entryway as 11°C (cooler than the rest of the residence at 19°C, due to the open inner door); algor was therefore not a reliable single indicator and I deferred to the rigor and livor estimate.

There was a single penetrating wound at the right anterior chest, approximately 4 cm inferior to the clavicle and 2 cm lateral to the sternum. The wound margins were clean and consistent with a single-edged blade approximately 2 cm in width. Track direction, on superficial inspection, was inferomedial. I deferred further assessment to autopsy.

Defensive injuries: a single linear abrasion to the dorsum of the left hand. No defensive lacerations to the palmar surfaces. Fingernails were clean and intact bilaterally; samples were taken under nail using standard ten-card protocol.

Clothing was undisturbed except at the wound site, where the shirt was darkened with blood over an area of approximately 18 cm by 14 cm. The shirt was unbuttoned to the third button; whether by the assailant or by the decedent in extremis is undetermined.

I authorised removal at 05:48. Body was transferred to St. Bartholomew's mortuary, where postmortem is scheduled for 09:00. I will attend.

Notes signed Dr. M. Eshe, 05:55, on-scene, transcribed at desk.
