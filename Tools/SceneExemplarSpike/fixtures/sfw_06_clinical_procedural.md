Inspection of structure 4710-A (eastbound carriageway, Carrick viaduct) conducted 09:30–14:15, 2026-05-13. Inspector: Patel, R. (Reg. SE-44219). Weather: dry, ambient 13°C, no precipitation in preceding 48 hours. Traffic: closed eastbound for inspection between 09:00 and 16:00 by prior arrangement with NHS Operations.

Visual inspection of deck soffit:

Span 1 (south abutment to pier 1). Soffit concrete in fair condition. Minor longitudinal cracking observed at six locations, none exceeding 0.3 mm in width. No spalling. No exposed reinforcement. Drainage: scupper 1.2 functional but slow; scupper 1.4 partially blocked by accumulated debris (cleared by Operations during inspection).

Span 2 (pier 1 to pier 2). Soffit concrete in fair-to-poor condition. Transverse cracking at one location (Bay 2-3, west girder) observed at 0.8 mm width, progressing into the upper third of the girder web. Recommended action: detailed half-cell potential survey within 90 days; consider inclusion in 2026 capital programme for cathodic protection assessment.

Bearings:

Pier 1, eastern bearing: laminated elastomeric. Visual inspection from access platform. No bulging, no sliding, no cracking of the elastomer. Anchor bolts intact, no corrosion staining observed.

Pier 1, western bearing: laminated elastomeric. As above, with minor surface oxidation on the anchor plate not penetrating the protective coating. No action required.

Pier 2 bearings: not inspected this cycle (scheduled 2027-Q1 per the rolling six-year programme).

Parapet and barrier:

Western parapet: galvanised steel railing, intact. Coating in fair condition; one section (chainage 124–134) shows surface rust over an area of approximately 0.4 m². Routine maintenance: spot-clean and re-coat within next planned maintenance window.

Eastern parapet: as western, no defects noted.

Drainage:

All deck-level scuppers tested by water-pouring method. Six of eight discharging freely. Two (1.4 and 3.2) partially blocked at inspection; debris cleared. Re-test within 30 days recommended.

Overall condition rating: General Condition Index 65 (Fair). Decreased from 71 at the 2024 inspection. Drivers of decrease: progression of the Bay 2-3 transverse crack and the soffit drainage degradation in Span 1. Both noted for the 2026 capital programme review.

Inspector recommendation: scheduled six-year cycle remains adequate at present. The Bay 2-3 transverse crack should be re-inspected at twelve months.

Report signed Patel, R., 14:35, on-site, transcribed at office 15:50.
